## 4. Origin and profile

**Brohangeta** is a pure “battleboarding construct”: it assumes the Dragon Ball setting’s strongest two-person fusion method (Potara) is applied to two already-fused super-composites. The mechanical reason this becomes absurd is stated directly in the Vegito source: Potara is described as **multiplication**, not addition, and also stated to be **superior to Fusion Dance**—meaning your stack is not merely “more fighters,” but “multipliers on multipliers.” citeturn11view0  
Psychologically, the fused personality would likely blend Goku/Vegeta’s tactical combat instincts, Gohan’s spikes (when serious), and Broly’s berserker pressure—yielding a fighter who can switch between high IQ dueling and brutal overwhelm. Gohan’s source explicitly frames his rage/berserk spikes as heavy boosts; Broly’s source shows escalating forms triggered by emotional thresholds. citeturn2view3turn2view1  

**Strange Visitor Superman** is explicitly framed as a life-spanning myth: the story is about Superman existing across eras, facing the loss of Lois, pursuing Mxyzptlk into the 5th Dimension, and continuing forward until the last moments of the universe. citeturn5view0  
His “combat philosophy” in this version is less about winning fights and more about **refusing to yield to entropy**—up to literally holding reality together and guiding life into a new universe at the end. citeturn5view0turn9view1turn9view4  

## 5. Powers and weaknesses

### Brohangeta: tools, vulnerabilities, swing factors
**Tools:**  
- Dragon Ball ki toolkit at fused peak: high-end striking, beam attacks, flight, aura pressure, and the demonstrated ability in the DBS Broly tier to break into/break out of “broken dimension” states and shatter a “barrier of reality” during beam clashes. citeturn12view0turn12view1turn2view1  
- Fusion mechanics advantage: Fusion Dance and Potara both explicitly grant more-than-additive boosts; Potara is described as multiplication and superior to Fusion Dance. citeturn10view0turn11view0  

**Vulnerabilities / swing factor:**  
- **Time limit risk** is huge. Fusion Dance is explicitly stated as 30 minutes; Potara is only permanent for Kaioshin and lasts one hour for non‑Kais, with notes that extreme power can shorten effective duration. citeturn10view0turn11view0  
- No explicit, scan-backed evidence in these sources that Dragon Ball ki gives reliable resistance to the kind of “cosmological manipulation” Strange Visitor Superman shows (universe-holding, spacetime tearing, out-running universal death). That means Brohangeta’s best plan is to win quickly via overwhelming force or incap. citeturn9view3turn9view4turn11view0  

### Strange Visitor Superman: tools, vulnerabilities, swing factors
**Tools:**  
- Dimensional agency: enters the 5th Dimension pursuit context (story summary) and is shown interacting with higher/strange spaces dangerous even to Mxyzptlk in concept. citeturn5view0turn7view0  
- Macro-endurance / immortality framing: “billions upon billions of years later” imagery + outliving everything in the universe. citeturn8view2turn5view0  
- Reality maintenance and multiplicity: divides into many copies and holds the universe together (scan + story framing). citeturn9view1turn5view0  
- Spacetime manipulation: “rip holes through spacetime” shown as a visual ripping/portal-like action. citeturn9view3  
- “Outruns the death of the universe” / survives the universe’s end state (visual portrayal). citeturn9view4turn7view0  

**Vulnerabilities / swing factor:**  
- The only plausible vulnerability is **getting one-shot before the ‘cosmic’ layers matter**—i.e., if Brohangeta can deliver immediate incap through raw speed and strike/beam lethality. But Strange Visitor Superman’s portrayal is explicitly beyond normal durability logic (“end of universe,” “holding universe together”), which makes that line very hard to argue from these sources. citeturn9view1turn9view4

## 8. Hypothetical fight - step by step

### 8.1 Opening

Brohangeta opens like a Dragon Ball top-tier: instantaneous pressure, multi-vector ki offense, and an attempt to end the fight before any “story-level” powers matter. The best evidence that this tier can force metaphysical breaks is Gogeta/Broly shattering a “barrier of reality” and destroying the dimension around them in collision exchanges. citeturn12view0turn12view1  
Strange Visitor Superman, by contrast, is not obliged to trade beams. Even early in the feat thread, he’s characterized by movement between spaces and surviving states that are “dangerous even for Mxyzptlk,” implying he can choose a flight path that is not just “run away in 3D space.” citeturn7view0turn8view1  

### 8.2 Mid-fight

If Strange Visitor Superman engages directly, Brohangeta can plausibly land. Dragon Ball fusions are extremely fast and can spam large-scale attacks at close range, and the fusion boost is explicitly more than additive (Fusion Dance) and multiplicative (Potara). citeturn10view0turn11view0  
But the mid-fight naturally shifts toward **outlasting** and **repositioning** because Strange Visitor Superman has multiple “I can survive the end of everything” anchors—billions of years later, copies holding the universe, outrunning universal death—while Brohangeta is mechanically time-bounded if we treat him as a non‑Kai Potara user. citeturn11view0turn8view2turn9view1turn9view4  

### 8.3 Turning points

**Turning point 1: Strange Visitor Superman chooses BFR/space-time tearing over tanking.**  
Spacetime ripping imagery suggests he can open holes/tears rather than absorb output on his body. If he can remove Brohangeta from the battlefield into a strange space, Brohangeta’s win condition collapses into “escape fast,” which is not guaranteed cross-verse. citeturn9view3  

**Turning point 2: The clock becomes the weapon.**  
Fusion Dance is stated to be 30 minutes; Potara is said to last an hour for non‑Kais (and can shorten with extreme power). Against an opponent whose story explicitly runs to the end of the universe, a one-hour cap is a catastrophic liability. citeturn10view0turn11view0turn9view4  

**Turning point 3: “Universe end” survivability vs “reality-barrier shatter.”**  
Brohangeta’s best “hax-ish” evidence is DBS-tier reality/dimensional shattering in clashes. Strange Visitor Superman’s best counter-evidence is not “I can shatter reality too,” but “I can survive the death of the universe and hold it together.” That’s a higher-order survivability claim than a collision-local ‘barrier crack.’ citeturn12view1turn9view1turn9view4
