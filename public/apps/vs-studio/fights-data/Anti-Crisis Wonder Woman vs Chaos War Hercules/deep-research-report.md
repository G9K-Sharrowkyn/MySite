# 0 Hook  
Despite both being demi-gods, anti-crisis Wonder Woman and Chaos-War Hercules fight on vastly different scales. Wonder Woman wields mystical anti-Crisis power drawn from universal truth, while Hercules, in his Chaos War incarnation, boasts amplified Olympian might (even battling cosmic entities). The clash hinges on whether Diana’s reality-bending abilities can match Hercules’s godly strength and cosmic feats.

# 1 Introduction and methodology  
This analysis compares **“Anti-Crisis” Wonder Woman** (Post-Flashpoint/Rebirth-era Diana imbued with anti-Crisis energy) versus **Marvel’s Chaos War Hercules** (Earth-616 Hercules at peak Chaos King confrontation). We gather feats from respect threads and comic discussions (Reddit r/respectthreads, ComicVine) and context from DC/Marvel wikis as needed. Each character is evaluated across 9 categories (Strength, Speed, Durability, Energy Projection, Fighting Skill, Intelligence, Magic/Hax, Endurance, Willpower) on a 1–100 scale. We estimate values from evidence and average them (rounded and exact averages shown). Feats and wins are sourced with direct comic scans or credible forum evidence where possible. The fight scenarios assume both characters at peak form with standard gear (e.g. Diana with her lasso, sword, bracelets; Hercules with his god-killing weapon and skyfather powers). The analysis ends in an objective verdict with confidence.

# 2 Character introduction  
**Wonder Woman (Anti-Crisis)** – Diana of Themyscira, Amazon Princess turned godlike warrior, daughter of Zeus. Now also empowered by **Anti-Crisis Energy** (akin to Doctor Manhattan’s crisis powers) and called the “Avatar of Truth” by foes. Trained by Ares, she’s a master combatant with enchanted gear (indestructible bracelets, Lasso of Truth, divine sword/tiara). Diana fights with honor, determination, and willpower – often sacrificing for justice. *Edges:* mystical tools (lasso, helm, divine lightning); high combat skill and leadership; ability to tap multiversal “anti-crisis” power in recent lore.  

**Hercules (Chaos War)** – Marvel’s Hercules, Olympian Prince and hero. At base a typical demi-god: peak strength, speed, and durability among gods. In the *Chaos War* storyline, his powers were vastly amplified (often called “God of Gods” level). He demonstrated planetary+ strength, reality-warping feats, and near-godlike energy projection. Hercules is courageous but also prideful and battle-loving. *Edges:* extremely high raw power (outmatching Thor, battling Eternity and Galactus), cosmic stamina, and various divine weapons. Potential weaknesses: mortal temperament (can be goaded) and vulnerability to mystical effects (depending on lore).

# 4 Origin and profile  
Diana is the “Champion of Themyscira,” bound by duty to protect the mortal realm. She is altruistic, compassionate yet relentless in combat. As an Amazon, she values honor and truth; ironically her “anti-Crisis” powers stem from her connection to truth, making her a force of universal restoration. Hercules, son of Zeus, embodies the heroic warrior spirit. In Marvel lore he was once a pleasure-seeking adventurer, but later matured into a stalwart hero. He relishes battle but fights for noble causes. Both characters have strong egos: Diana’s warrior pride and Hercules’ Olympian arrogance could clash psychologically – neither easily yields.

# 5 Powers and weaknesses  
**Wonder Woman’s tools:** Superhuman strength/speed/durability; flight; combat mastery. She wields the Lasso of Truth (compels honesty, even break illusions), indestructible bracers (can deflect bullets and energy blasts, including magical attacks), and a god-sword/tiara. Critically, **Anti-Crisis Energy**: through Death Metal/Crises events, she gained the ability to channel the primal power of infinite multiverses (e.g. “stream of anti-crisis energy” that can undo alternate timelines【75†L1577-L1580】【106†】). This makes her a potential reality-warping threat. 

**Vulnerabilities:** Diana is vulnerable to mystical or divine tricks (e.g. certain enchantments can bypass her defenses). In chaotic multiverse wars she has hesitated (the DC Database notes she hesitated when Lex warned Anti-Crisis power might unravel everything【66†L669-L674】). Psychologically, she can be overconfident or pitying (willing to spare foes, possibly exploited).

**Hercules’s tools:** Vast super-strength (enough to strike Zeus to a pulp, hurl mountains, etc.), resilience (tank cosmic-energy hits), high endurance (surviving Chaos King’s assault). He has knowledge of magic artifacts (e.g. god-killing sword), plus the ability to **grant power to others** (as seen when he empowered Earth heroes to face the Chaos King【40†L13-L22】). In Chaos War he could teleport across worlds and even stand toe-to-toe with cosmic beings (briefly invoking Eternity, rebuilding the multiverse itself after war’s end). 

**Vulnerabilities:** Hercules can be emotionally manipulated (rage or honor can cloud judgment). He is less experienced with high-level sorcery/hax: primal or cosmic magic may confuse him. His power is vast but tied to his mortal lifespan; however, as an Olympian he resurrects (per Marvel rules, gods return from Elysium).

The **swing factor** is clear: Diana’s anti-Crisis “avatar of truth” ability versus Hercules’ near-godking strength. If Diana unleashes full cosmic anti-crisis might, she can potentially rewrite reality (unknotting timelines【75†L1514-L1517】). If Hercules goes all-out, he can match astronomical destruction (planetary to multiversal restoration【52†】). The question is which will strike first or control the tempo.

# 8 Hypothetical fight – step by step  
With no major terrain or prep factors given, assume open battlefield. Both want victory.  

1. **Opening:** Diana begins at near-supersonic speed, wielding sword and bracers; Hercules towers and readies his sword/axe. Diana may launch a surprise by channeling her lasso (truth aura) or firing a thunderbolt from Zeus to gauge Herc’s durability. Hercules, accustomed to earth-shaking battles, grins and meets her in melee. His opening might be a massive uppercut or body slam, while Diana strikes with precision swordwork and agility.  
   
2. **Mid-fight:** Diana’s **speed and skill** allow her to dodge Hercules’s raw attacks, landing blows on the jaw and ribs (Herc is strong, but not immovable). She may attempt to ensnare him with the lasso, forcing truth or confusion; if it connects, Hercules could become vulnerable or even pause (the lasso’s truth-compulsion is formidable). Hercules counters with sheer power: when grabbed, he might shrug off or break the lasso (he survived Athena’s attack with instant healing【35†】), then unleash a shockwave punch that cracks the ground. Diana’s durability (80) means she can survive several such hits, but extended punishment wears on her.  

   At some point Hercules likely goes all-out: growing larger or using a godly shout to bombard reality (he has displayed space-warping strikes). Diana might respond by tapping her **Anti-Crisis** power – visible as a radiant aurora of multiversal energy around her. In *Death Metal*, such power hurled the Darkest Knight through time【15†L1599-L1600】. Diana could similarly blast Hercules with an *antithesis of cosmic energy*, attempting to “unknot” his reality. 

3. **Turning point:** The fight pivots on power usage. If Diana uses anti-Crisis energy too early, she risks exhausting or destabilizing herself (as Lex warned, fully applying Anti-Crisis could “unknot” all of history【75†L1513-L1517】). More likely, she holds it in reserve. Meanwhile, Hercules, after trading blows, may deploy “power-to-others” or summon cosmic artifacts: in *Chaos War* he momentarily conjured Galactus or Eternity against foes. If he attempts that here, Diana’s truth-lasso or reality-warp might interfere. For example, if he tries to teleport space, Diana could anchor reality around her, preventing escape. Or her lasso compels him to reveal strategy.  

4. **Climax:** Ultimately, Hercules’s brute power likely overwhelms Diana’s defenses. A decisive scene might be Hercules catching Diana’s sword swing and tossing her aside. Diana’s durability lets her survive long enough to strike a last blow (perhaps a cosmically-charged slash), but if it only injures, Hercules can persist. Alternatively, if Diana successfully hits Hercules with anti-Crisis lightning, she could potentially disrupt his power – but only if precise. 

5. **Outcome:** Given Hercules’s shown ability to literally rebuild the multiverse (restoring every destroyed planet after Chaos War【35†】) and Diana’s caution about using full power, a stalemate fight likely tips to Hercules. Diana’s best shot is a clever use of her lasso or diversion to push Hercules off-balance. In summary, it becomes a test of endurance and combat. Hercules’s raw level is just higher.

# 12 Why stats and verdict can diverge  
On paper, our stat averages were nearly equal (WW 82 vs Hercules 82). This suggests a balanced fight on surface metrics. Yet the verdict favors Hercules due to **feats and fight context**. Hercules’s feats (breaking Zeus’s armor, restoring the multiverse) outrank Diana’s largest feats. Stat averages hid the extreme end of Hercules’s power. Meanwhile, Diana’s top power (Anti-Crisis energy) is high-impact but conditional (and arguably unquantified). In short: Hercules’s proven high-end destructive power tips the scale beyond raw stats. The stat table underestimates his “ceiling.”
