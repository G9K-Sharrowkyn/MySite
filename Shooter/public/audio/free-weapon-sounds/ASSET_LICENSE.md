# Free Weapon Sound Effects

Source package: [Free Weapon Sound Effects](https://www.fab.com/listings/1697af22-7e2a-410e-b8c0-88239216520d)

The selected handgun, assault-rifle and shotgun WAV files were converted to OGG
for browser playback. The sounds remain governed by the license attached to the
Fab listing; this file records their provenance and is not a replacement for
the marketplace license terms.

The original downloaded ZIP remains outside the game bundle in the project
root.
